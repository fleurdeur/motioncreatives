Preset Finder

Pull preset links and Drive XML files out of a TikTok caption, bio and comments.

  1. Paste a TikTok video link
  2. The scanner reads the caption, bio, link in bio, comments and replies
  3. Preset links and Google Drive files (like XML) are picked out and sorted into groups
  4. Copy the one you need. New finds also go to the Preset Library for review
